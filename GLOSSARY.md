# Music curation

A listener describes the music they want for an activity and plays the resulting playlist in order.

## Language

**Listener**

The person choosing a playlist to accompany an activity.

**Recording**

A particular recorded version of a song. An original and a remix are separate recordings, each with its own tempo and musical key.

**Cultural fit**

How well a recording evokes the scene, mood, or themes in the listener’s prompt. A review passage about the song carries more weight than a general description of its album.

**Transition**

The change a listener hears as one recording ends and the next begins. Its suitability depends on the activity and the progression the listener requested.
